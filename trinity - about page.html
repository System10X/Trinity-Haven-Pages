My story

A psychologist who believes in your capacity to grow

Hi, I'm Winnie. I became a psychologist because I believe deeply in people — in their resilience, their wisdom, and their capacity to create meaningful change in their lives, even when that feels impossible.

Over more than 17 years, I've had the privilege of sitting with people from every walk of life — those navigating the depths of grief, parents learning to understand their child's world, professionals quietly struggling behind closed doors, and young people finding their footing. Every person has taught me something.

I work collaboratively. That means I don't position myself as the expert on your life — you are. My role is to ask the right questions, offer new perspectives, and walk alongside you as you discover what's possible. I believe in meeting people where they are, without judgement and without rushing.

At Trinity Haven Psychology, my goal is simple: to create a space where you feel genuinely safe, heard, and empowered to take the next step — whatever that looks like for you.

How I work

My approach to psychology

Therapy works best when it's built around you — not a script.

Collaborative

We work together as partners. You bring knowledge of your own life; I bring clinical expertise. Neither is more important than the other.

Strengths-based

I look for what's working before focusing on what isn't. You already have more inner resources than you may realise — we find them together.

Evidence-based

Every approach I use is grounded in research. I stay current through ongoing professional development to ensure you receive the best available care.

Culturally inclusive

I recognise that culture shapes how we understand ourselves and our struggles. I honour the values, beliefs, and backgrounds each person brings.

Holistic

I look at the whole person — not just symptoms. Your relationships, environment, physical health, and sense of identity all matter in our work together.

Pace-led by you

There is no rush and no fixed script. We move at a pace that feels right for you, building safety and trust before going anywhere that feels tender.

Therapeutic approaches

The tools I use

I draw on a range of evidence-based therapeutic approaches, always chosen to suit the individual — not the other way around. Here are the main frameworks I work with:

I am committed to continuous professional development and regularly update my practice with the latest clinical research.

Explore our services
CBT

Cognitive Behaviour Therapy

Helps identify and shift unhelpful thought patterns and behaviours that maintain distress. Practical, goal-focused, and highly effective for anxiety and depression.

ACT

Acceptance & Commitment Therapy

Builds psychological flexibility — learning to accept what can't be controlled while committing to actions aligned with your values.

MI

Motivational Interviewing

A collaborative conversation style that helps you explore your own motivations for change — without pressure or judgement.

CW

Chairwork Psychotherapy

An experiential approach that helps process difficult emotions, inner conflicts, and relational patterns through active, embodied techniques.

SFT

Solution-Focused Therapy

Orients toward your strengths and existing solutions rather than problems. Future-focused and empowering — particularly effective for shorter-term goals.

SBA

Strengths-Based Approach

Identifies and amplifies your natural capacities, resilience, and resources as the foundation for lasting change and wellbeing.
