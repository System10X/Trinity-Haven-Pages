Do I need a referral to see a psychologist?+

No — you can book directly without a referral. However, a Mental Health Care Plan (MHCP) from your GP allows you to claim a $101.55 Medicare rebate for up to 10 sessions per year.

What happens in the first session?+

Your first session is a gentle, unhurried conversation about your current situation, background, and goals. We'll discuss confidentiality, answer any questions, and begin developing a personalised treatment plan together.

Do you offer telehealth sessions?+

Yes — all sessions are available face to face at our Norwest clinic or via telehealth online. Medicare rebates apply to both formats.

What is the cancellation policy?+

We ask for at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. A $50 fee applies for late cancellations and $100 for no-shows without notice.
